The Authority Sprint · A 45-day build

Somewhere right now, someone is deciding about you.From photos that no longer read as you.

The Sprint rebuilds the version of you the internet finds, until it matches the person they just met. One story and one face, in 45 days.

In plain terms: an audit and a Recognition Session, an art-directed portrait session, a produced interview with short-form edits, and three published features. Forty-five days, built as one engagement.

What to expect

Here's exactly how the 45 days run.

Day 1
Onboarding

We look at everywhere you show up

A short intake, then an audit of your site, your profiles and what a search of your name turns up. We agree on the story we want the market to recognize.

Week 1
See

The Recognition Session

The conversation that pulls out your story and what makes you distinct. Every visual concept comes from it.

Day 7
Say

Your map is in your hands

Your audit, recorded as a walkthrough of everywhere you show up. A one-page authority map: where you're visible now, where the gaps are, and the positioning to lead with.

Your editorial angle, with the two sentences people should know you by, and fixes for every profile. For teams, a curated list of further publications and podcasts with a pitch draft ready to send.

Days 8–45
Show

The capture and the build

Your portrait session is directed end to end, with a behind-the-scenes photographer on set. The podcast episode is recorded and produced, and the placements go into motion.

Day 45
Delivery

It lands together

Three published features with your editorial portraits. A produced podcast episode with branded cover art. Short-form content circulating through collaborative posts. A social presence that tells one story, and one clear place to send anyone who wants to know more.

Day 46
Stand

The review

What we built, how people are responding and where the best openings are. Some clients carry it on alone. Others go deeper into the Icon Method.

We commit to the work above, on a timeline we agree together, at the standard you've seen on this site.

We can't promise how many people reach out once it's live. That depends on your market and how widely the work travels. What the Sprint does is create the conditions for recognition and for the right inquiries.

And it starts with a conversation, so we can both tell whether it's the right fit.
