Questions

Is it right for you?

Who is this for?

Founders, experts and thought leaders who've already shown people will follow them and pay for what they know. Often you're good at too many things to say what you do in one sentence.

Who isn't it for?

Anyone who wants an image they haven't earned. We don't invent credentials or build authority that isn't there. And if you need convincing that this work matters, it's probably not the right time.

Do I need new photos first?

No. Photos come after the Recognition Session. What you think you need on day one is usually not what we end up making.

Is the Recognition Session therapy?

No. It can go deep, and people sometimes cry. It's a creative conversation, and it never stands in for a doctor or a therapist.

What happens when it's over?

We sit down and look at what we built, how people are responding and where the best openings are. Then you decide whether to carry it on alone or join Icon Circle.
