Dear friend,

If your photos are a few years old, if your matches go quiet after two messages, or if the first date goes fine and then she stops answering, this letter is for you.

My name is Emma Parven. I founded Radius, the New York app that shows you who's into you in the room you're already standing in, and I host dating events in this city. I know what women notice, and I know how a man should show up.

A few months ago, at an event I helped host, a guy told someone on my team that he'd pay anyone to fix his dating profile. He'd asked a bunch of people and nobody had taken him up on it. So I texted him, sat down with him, and we rebuilt the whole thing together.

I don't believe men like him need a new personality. They need someone who will tell them the truth about their photos, their prompts, what they wear and what they talk about, and then help them fix it. Most of their friends won't say it, and the women they match with certainly won't.

I'd rather you meet her in real life, in real time. That's why I built Radius. But if apps are where you're dating right now, or walking up to a stranger feels like too much, let's make sure the man she meets is the same man she saw on the screen.

And the goal is bigger than a first date. A better profile can get you that. What I care about is the second one, which is why we work on the profile, the date, and what comes after it.
