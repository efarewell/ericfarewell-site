The Royals Mastermind

Everyone brings you their problems. Who do you bring yours to?

Somewhere between the first company and this one, I became the person everyone brings things to. That is a role I chose and mostly love. What took me longer to notice is that it leaves you nowhere to set your own weight down. This room is where I set mine.

What it is

A room for builders doing meaningful work.

The Royals Mastermind is ongoing. Not a course with an end date, not a program you graduate from. It is a group of founders holding honest conversations about growth, leadership and legacy, and staying in each other's business long enough to be useful.

This is not about more hustle. It is about deeper presence. Most of the people here can already run hard. What they are after is doing it without burning down the parts of their life they built the company to protect.

We meet, we tell the truth, and nobody performs. That is the whole design.

Fit

It is not for everyone, and I would rather say so.

This is for you if

  • You have built something real and you are carrying more of it alone than you admit.
  • You want to scale with clarity rather than by adding hours.
  • You will say the true thing in a room of peers, and you want to be asked about it later.
  • You are looking for people who stay, rather than a call you attend once.

Skip it if

  • You want tactics and templates. There are cheaper places to get those.
  • You want me one to one. That is a different container, and it costs considerably more.
  • You want someone to do the work for you. Nobody here will.
  • You are not ready to be honest in front of people who will remember what you said.
