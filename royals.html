Royals

Success can build the company.
It cannot tell you what the life is for.

Royals is a private room for founders, coaches, and leaders who know how to make things happen and are no longer willing to let revenue be the only measure of a life. We work where freedom, fear, family, leadership, the nervous system, and legacy meet the company.

The whole thing can come in

A business conversation that can follow the truth.

We talk about money, roles, hiring, delegation, sales, systems, and the decisions that keep waiting. We also make room for the fear, identity, relationship, grief, or state that has never stayed neatly inside the business for long.

I bring what I see and what I have lived. The people in the room bring their own experience. None of us becomes the final authority on another person's life, and the decision remains with the person who has to live it.

The practice

Tell the truth. Choose a move. Return to what happened.

01

See what is here

We begin with what happened, what the evidence shows, and what the person in the room notices.

02

Settle enough to choose

We notice the state a decision is coming from before calling the reaction a strategy.

03

Separate fact from story

An event, the meaning attached to it, the fear around it, and the responsibility inside it are not always the same thing.

04

Change the design

The insight reaches a seat, system, boundary, calendar, conversation, or decision.

05

Return

We come back to what changed, what did not, and where the pressure moved.

The 90-minute Experience

The Experience is a real room, not a disguised sales call. Guests may listen and are not required to disclose anything. When a real question and the room meet, there may be space to work with it. There is also time to understand the Accelerator.

The room is not recorded, and what is shared is not distributed afterward. Attendance begins with an application and human review. An application does not promise an invitation.

The Royals Accelerator

The Accelerator is twelve live calls across 90 days. We work with the frameworks that have helped me move from being required by the company toward more freedom, then ask what freedom is for: the role, relationships, work, and legacy somebody actually wants to build.

12 weeks · Weekly calls · $10,000

It is a complete experience, not a waiting room for something more exclusive. Cohort dates are shared when the next room is ready.

Continue

The Royals Mastermind

The Mastermind is the continuing room for people who have completed the Accelerator and are invited to remain. Time matters here. People can see what a new room would miss because they know the person, the company, the family, the patterns, and what has already been tried.

The Mastermind is not open to direct application. Completion creates the possibility of an invitation, not an entitlement to one.

The wisdom in the room

I am not meant to be the answer to every question.

The people in Royals have built, bought, sold, repaired, and lost real things. They have led companies, teams, families, communities, and themselves through seasons that did not fit inside a framework.

My responsibility is to hold the conditions, offer what I see, share what I have lived, and help the right experience find the person who needs it. We witness before we fix. We share experience without pretending another person's life can be copied.

Where it may fit

A willingness to be unfinished.

The room is built for people who have made or accepted real responsibility and want insight to reach the company and the life around it. It asks for participation, honesty, respect for privacy, and a willingness to return to what happened.

It is less useful as a passive course library, a prestigious network, a place to sell to members, or a room asked to own another person's decision.

A private beginning

A tool may be enough for the first question.

The Guided Start offers one practice based on what needs attention now. If the work creates a question that should not be carried alone, the Experience remains available.
