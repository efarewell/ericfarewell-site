FROM PRESTON, JEDDY AND ERIC
THE FOUNDERS OF THE STANDARD

Brother,

Last March, at Unleash, a Marine Corps veteran had what he later called a huge PTSD moment. He hadn't realized how much he was carrying that weekend until Preston challenged him on something he'd been told his whole life: "You deflect with jokes."

So he set the jokes down, and what came up was fear. Fear of who he'd been in the Marine Corps, and of so many other things. He ended up in the middle of the room, surrounded by men, most of whom were meeting him for the first time that weekend.

Not one of them was afraid of him, and not one of them judged him. Preston stayed right with him, bringing him back into his body and asking him to open his eyes and look around. Eric had a hand on his chest, and another brother had a hand there too. He cried like he never had before.

He'd never been held by another man, his father included. Months later, telling the story on one of our calls, he said hard things still happen, but everything in his business and his life has gone up since that weekend, and that he needs these men constantly.

Most of us arrive the way he did, carrying more than we've admitted, good at keeping things light, and used to being the one everyone else leans on. Unleash is two days built for setting that down in front of men who can hold it.

That's Unleash the Beast. It happens once a year, and on February 6 and 7 we're doing it again in Buda.

WHAT IT IS

Two days. Men only. All in.

Unleash is two full days of physical, emotional and spiritual work on Preston's land in Buda, just south of Austin. It's the one event in The Standard's year that's open to men outside the brotherhood. Our members come as part of their year, and many of them carry real leadership in the room, so every man who walks in for the first time is met by men who've been doing this work all year.

We built it for the man who's read the books and done the therapy and still feels the distance between what he knows and how he lives. We believe that distance closes in a room full of men, with your body in it and no way to watch from the back.

Half of the weekend is new this year, and some of it works best met fresh. Here's what we can tell you.

We move

This is physical work. We push, sweat and get on the ground together, so wear clothes you're happy to ruin.

We breathe

Long, deep breathwork that gets underneath the thinking mind, which is where most of what we carry is stored.

We feel it, held

We go into fear, anger, grief and shame, and every man is held while he does. You'll be pushed past comfortable and never left alone there.

We laugh

The deep work comes with a lot of laughter, and that laughter is what lets men stay in the room for the hard parts.

We leave with brothers

Men walk out of Unleash with new numbers in their phones and brothers they can call on Monday.

We go home

Sunday evening you drive home to the people you love, and that's where the weekend is meant to show up first.

HOW THE TWO DAYS RUN

Saturday takes the mask off. Sunday finds what's under it.

DAY ONE · SATURDAY

We start in a circle and get out of our heads and into our bodies. From there we go straight at authority, fear, failure and shame, the things most men carry alone for years, and we carry that load together, literally. You will get dirty.

The day closes with breathwork and a practice of honoring the people in your life who've earned it. Then there's homework Saturday night.

DAY TWO · SUNDAY

Authority again, from the inside this time. Intuition work with blindfolds on. Grief. How we relate to the feminine, and what we do with our sexual energy.

Then a final ritual we won't spoil here, and a debrief so the weekend goes home with you. The real work starts Monday.
