The whole life comes in

I stopped believing the business could be separated from the person building it.

I have built from ambition, fear, speed, usefulness, love, and the desire to create something that mattered. Some of it worked beautifully. Some of it cost more than I understood while I was inside it.

The work changed when I began paying attention to the state behind a decision, the story wrapped around it, the system reproducing it, and the life that would have to carry the result. I still care about the company, the money, the seat, and the strategy. I also care about the marriage, the children, the body, the fear, and what any of it is for.

A place to begin

The first useful move may be smaller than a coaching relationship.

I have built a handful of free practices from questions that kept appearing in my companies, writing, decisions, and coaching rooms. The short Guided Start asks what needs attention and offers one place to begin.

It is not a diagnosis, and the recommendation is not a verdict. It is one way to begin working with what is already here.

Writing and speaking

I write down what I am still learning to live.

The books, essays, talks, and rooms come from the same place. They hold what I have learned through companies, family, grief, fear, recovery, adventure, relationships, and the life I am still living.

I do not believe my experience becomes another person's instruction simply because I wrote it down. I can share what I have seen, the choices I made, and what happened next. The reader or the room decides what belongs to them.
