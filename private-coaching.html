Private coaching

Some seasons need a room
with fewer people in it.

I have had parts of my life that would not stay inside the category I gave them. A business problem reached my marriage. An old fear became a management pattern. A body I kept overriding eventually made the decision for me.

Begin a private inquiry
Across the whole thing

The work can follow what is true.

Private coaching is where I work with one person across a company, a transition, a relationship, a decision, the cost of being needed, or a life that no longer feels like the one they meant to build.

I may ask a direct question, share what I have lived, or help design the operational change that makes an insight visible. I do not become the final authority on another person's life, and I do not take ownership of a choice they have to live with.

What the relationship makes room for
01

Privacy

Some decisions need room before they are ready for a team, a peer group, or a public explanation.

02

Continuity

The conversation can continue across the company, the body, the relationship, and the week that follows.

03

Design

An insight can become a role, boundary, calendar change, conversation, handoff, or experiment with a date on it.

The container

The full commitment should be clear before a decision.

Private coaching is limited because the relationship requires attention and continuity. The current term, session rhythm, between-session access, in-person elements, and investment are explained during the inquiry process before anyone is asked to decide.

I would rather leave a gap than publish details that are no longer true. The application begins with the situation and helps me decide whether a conversation seems useful.

Scope

Perspective is not treatment.

This work may include fear, grief, trauma, relationships, the nervous system, and the body. I work from lived experience and coaching practice. Private coaching is not medical, legal, financial, or mental-health treatment.

When something belongs with a licensed professional or crisis resource, involving the person qualified for that responsibility may be the most useful next move.

Begin with what is happening

The polished version can wait.

The application asks what is happening, what has already been tried, and what remains. I read each inquiry before deciding whether a conversation seems useful.
