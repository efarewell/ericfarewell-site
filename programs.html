The path into Royals

You should not have to buy the room
before you have felt the room.

Royals begins with a private, unrecorded 90-minute Experience. If the work and the people fit, the Accelerator is the next step. The ongoing Mastermind is never a direct application.

01 · Experience the room

Ninety private minutes before a larger decision.

The Royals Experience is a real call with current members and invited guests. People may listen. Nobody is required to disclose anything. The room is not recorded, and the stories shared inside it are not distributed afterward.

Applications are reviewed by a person. Applying does not promise an invitation.
