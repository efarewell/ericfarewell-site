# Own-hand practice

Whole-mark attempts from Trace, Copy, Own it, and explicitly kept open practice
can be compared in the open tab. The first and latest snapshots for each design
are held in memory, with actual stroke geometry and honest guidance labels.
Letter drills do not enter the comparison. No quality or improvement score is
claimed. A reflection choice gives one concrete detail to try next.

Pictures are not uploaded, included in redraw requests, or persisted to browser
storage. Refreshing clears them. Forgetting pictures does not reset practice
counts. Replacing revised artwork or restoring the original clears that design's
comparison. Downloads are local black-on-white PNGs.

Focus mode hides the sidebar without changing progress. Its button and Escape
restore the path. Paper mode offers a printable sheet with fading guides, blank
lines, and a 72 mm reference area to try at an everyday size. The paper checklist
is a personal check, not an award or assertion of mastery.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, focused unit tests, all prior August lesson,
discovery and monochrome tests, plus site integrity checks. Browser QA used real
pointer drags for an AF example, verified trace/copy captions and optional
reflection, and checked focus controls. Saved comparison-file retrieval stalled
in the browser testing helper; the PNG export contract is tested, but that native
download path and physical printing remain device QA. No paid AI call was made.

All prior favorites, artwork storage, approvals and practice counts are preserved.
This release does not add a public navigation link or change the redraw service,
family code, account permissions or costs.
