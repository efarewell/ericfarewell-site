The questions stopped.

Somewhere between the first hire and the person you are now, people quit asking you real questions. Your team brings you problems shaped like requests. Your friends accept the first answer.

The Solo Hot Seat is a twenty-five minute self-guided session built from the room I run for founders: a two-minute drop-in, three rounds of questions that get harder on purpose, and one sentence you'll say out loud before it's over.

Pen and paper required. Performance optional, and unwelcome.

The drop-in

Why This Exists

I started my first company at thirteen, and I've spent most of my life since then being the person other people bring their problems to. It took me a long time to notice what that arrangement was costing me.

I run a room where that arrangement doesn't hold. Founders, no audience to perform for, and questions that don't accept the first answer. This is a piece of that room, for one. Phone in another room, door closed, twenty-five minutes where nothing needs anything from you.

Twenty-five minutes with the questions nobody asks you anymore

Free, and not for everyone. If sitting alone with real questions sounds like a waste of an afternoon, it probably is, for you. If something in you leaned forward, that's worth noticing.
