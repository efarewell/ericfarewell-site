The Royal Measure

Help me test what we are building.

I am building one assessment that can hold more of a founder in one place: how you naturally work, what has your attention, and how your life is arranged right now.

This is an alpha. Some questions may need better language. Some results may feel exact, and some may miss. That is why I am asking you to take it now. I need your experience before I pretend the instrument is finished.

You can take it with me or on your own. It is divided into four measured sessions, and every completed answer is saved. You can close the page, come back later, and continue from the same place. After those sessions, you may add the Symbolic Chart as an optional fifth layer.

  1. Session 1How you naturally work
  2. Session 2What currently has your attention
  3. Session 3How you are meeting what is expected
  4. Session 4The patterns that travel with you
  5. Optional layerThe Symbolic Chart

Your name and email are saved in Farewell Holdings’ GoHighLevel account so I know who is testing. Your assessment answers stay in the private assessment system. They are not copied into your GoHighLevel contact record.

Before you begin

Use the result as a mirror, not a verdict.

It is provisional.The Royal Measure has not been validated or normed. The result is a working hypothesis for reflection and coaching.
It is voluntary.Taking it, stopping, or declining has no effect on membership, coaching, access, pricing, or your place in any room.
It is not a high-stakes tool.Do not use it for hiring, diagnosis, eligibility, or any decision that determines someone else’s opportunity.
Your feedback matters.Each session ends with a short optional feedback prompt. Tell me what was clear, what was tiring, and what did not make sense.
The Symbolic Chart is separate.If you choose it, your birth details are encrypted, kept out of GoHighLevel, and do not change any measured result. You can delete that layer without deleting the assessment.
Evidence line: invitation-only alpha, E0. Not validated, not normed, not diagnostic, and not for hiring.
