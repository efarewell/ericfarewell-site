Coaching

The business problem is rarely
only a business problem.

A role can outgrow the person who built it. Fear can become a management system. Revenue can keep climbing while freedom, presence, and purpose quietly move the other way. I work with founders and leaders where the company, the body, the relationships, and the life around the work meet.

What the work follows

We do not force the truth into one category.

01

The pattern beneath the problem

We look at what happened, what keeps returning, and where speed, fear, identity, or the need to be needed may be shaping the company.

02

The state behind the decision

More information does not always create more clarity. We slow the noise enough to notice the body, separate fact from story, and choose from a steadier place.

03

The change that reaches Tuesday

Insight becomes a conversation, boundary, role, handoff, calendar change, or experiment we can return to and examine.
