Carved surrounds

The surround is where most fireplaces stop trying.

Stone gets cut flat, a mantel gets mounted and everyone moves on to the kitchen. We'd rather carve it. Working with stone shops and CNC fabricators, we can carve limestone or granite into a mantel, a full surround or the entire chimney breast, with relief deep enough that the surface changes as the light moves across the room.

We manage the design, the fabricator and the install as one package alongside the firebox and venting, so the stone and the fire behind it are built by people who talk to each other.

  • Carved stone mantelsCorbels, moldings and rosettes cut in classic profiles or to your architect's own drawing.
  • Family crests & bas-reliefThe client's mark or a scene worked into the stone, the kind of detail that stays with the house.
  • Texas limestoneStacked, cut or carved, in the stone that defines this part of the state.
  • Carved panelingCNC-cut panels that wrap the firebox and carry a pattern up the wall.
  • Full-height chimney breastsFloor to ceiling, treated as one continuous piece.

Floating fireplaces

A fire that hangs in the middle of the room.

A suspended fireplace is a steel orb hung from its own flue, with the fire burning inside it. Because it lives in open air instead of a wall, the flame can be seen from more than one side, and it becomes the center of gravity for an open plan, a great room or a restored mid-century home. We're seeing more of them in Austin homes every year, and we source and install them start to finish.

Openings
Forward-facing, or see-through from both sides
Fuel
Gas, wood or bioethanol, depending on the model and the home
Mounting
Hung from the flue, with the pipe cut on site to your ceiling height
Placement
Out in the room, where the fire can be seen from every side
Partners
Vita and select specialty makers

Plan it early. A suspended unit needs the structure above it and the flue route worked out before the ceiling closes. Call us at framing, not at trim.
